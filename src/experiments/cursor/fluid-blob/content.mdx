A squashy purple blob that follows your cursor. It stretches when you move and squashes back when you slow down, with six ghost copies trailing behind from a reused pool, so there's no extra allocation per frame.

**Usage**

```tsx
import CursorFluidBlob from './CursorFluidBlob'

<CursorFluidBlob
  points={8}
  baseRadius={28}
  lerpPos={0.10}
  lerpRadius={0.18}
  stretchAmt={0.55}
  wobbleAmt={2.2}
  wobbleSpeed={0.055}
  color="90,50,210"
  trailCount={6}
/>
```

**Props**

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `points` | `number` | `8` | Number of vertices around the blob's perimeter. More points give a smoother outline |
| `baseRadius` | `number` | `28` | Resting radius of the blob in pixels |
| `lerpPos` | `number` | `0.10` | How quickly the blob's position eases toward the cursor (0 to 1, higher is snappier) |
| `lerpRadius` | `number` | `0.18` | How quickly each point's radius eases toward its wobble target |
| `stretchAmt` | `number` | `0.55` | How much the blob elongates based on movement speed |
| `wobbleAmt` | `number` | `2.2` | Amplitude of the idle outline oscillation, in pixels |
| `wobbleSpeed` | `number` | `0.055` | Speed of the idle oscillation |
| `color` | `string` | `'90,50,210'` | Fill color as an `"r,g,b"` triplet, without the `rgb()` wrapper|
| `trailCount` | `number` | `6` | Number of ghost copies in the smear trail |
